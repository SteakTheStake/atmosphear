# Third-party audio

The recordings in `assets/library/`, including edited, trimmed, mixed-down, and looped derivatives, are **excluded from this repository's MIT license**. Copyright remains with the respective recording owners. The code license does not grant a separate stock-audio license, a right to claim authorship of the original recordings, or permission to redistribute the sounds as a standalone sound-effects collection.

Only recordings referenced by the playable Godot scenes and sound palette are included. [The audio manifest](assets/library/manifest.json) records original local source paths, source SHA-256 values, processing, and output hashes. [The used-audio inventory](assets/library/used_audio.json) records the scene/resource references. The original download archives and unused recordings are not part of the repository.
